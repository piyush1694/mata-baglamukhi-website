॥ ह्लीं ॥

Known as Pitambara Devi, Maa Baglamukhi is worshipped as one of the ten Mahavidyas. The golden-yellow colour associated with her worship is a sign of auspiciousness, clarity and devotion.

01 The story

When the storm
grew quiet

An ancient legend remembers the moment Maa Baglamukhi appeared to restore balance to a world in turmoil.

In the Satya Yuga, a great storm threatened to overwhelm creation. Deeply concerned, Lord Vishnu turned to prayer and performed austerities on the shore of Haridra Sarovar, the lake of turmeric. Pleased by his devotion, the Goddess appeared from its golden waters in the form of Baglamukhi.

Her presence stilled the storm and brought the universe back to calm. In another telling, she quieted the harmful words of the demon Madan, becoming a symbol of restraint, inner steadiness and the power of thoughtful speech.

These accounts are part of a living devotional tradition. For devotees, her story is a reminder to seek composure and clarity through prayer.
